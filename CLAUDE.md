# Asset Living build

Interview build exercise for **VP, Business Operations & AI Enablement** at Asset Living (third-party property manager, Houston). Deliverable: a working prototype for one of three scenarios, a 5–10 min narrated video, and a live 15-min demo to a panel from Asset Living and New Mountain Capital. It is framed as one module of an AI-first operating layer above the PMS (pillars: Residents / Operations / Back Office / Owners).

## The prototype (Scenario B, built)

`interview_demo/asset-os/`: the Close Assistant, a static app inside an "Asset Living OS" platform shell. Open `index.html` directly; `python3 tools/serve.py` (port 8765, caching off) is only a convenience while editing. See its `README.md` for the walkthrough, findings, assumptions and limits.

- `js/engine.js` reads the workbook and computes everything: the checks, commentary drafts, number and cause checks, scoring. Thresholds are the config at the top of the file and can be changed on the **Ontology & rules** tab.
- `js/app.js` (Close Assistant screens, decisions, audit trail, outputs), `js/shell.js` (sidebar, executive view, Payables concept) and `js/chat.js` (Ask panel) only render.
- `js/data-pack.js` is the data pack, embedded. Rebuild it with `python3 tools/embed_data_pack.py path/to/pack.xlsx`.
- `js/answer-key.js` holds the expected results, generated by `tools/answer_key.py` (the same rules written a second time in Python). After changing a rule, regenerate it and run `node tools/test_engine.js` (needs `npm install --prefix tools xlsx@0.18.5` once). Expected: 53 of 53 caught, 0 missed, 0 false alarms.
- `css/app.css` is compiled Tailwind. After changing class names or the config: `cd tailwind && npm install && npm run build`.
- AI features (drafts, the Ask panel, invoice intake) are simulated by templates over real computed figures; no model is called.
- Decisions are kept in the browser's local storage. **Reset the demo** on the Outputs tab clears them.

## From the interview (first-hand, outranks the research)

- Property accounting is several hundred offshore staff plus an onshore team, across three PMSs with different accounting workflows; much manual translation and bank reconciliation.
- Poor accounting loses owners fastest: late financials, wrong-account postings, surprise true-ups after a distribution. Cutting errors and keeping clients is valued above cutting headcount. Lead with errors caught, then time saved.

## Facts to keep in mind

- 446K units (NMHC 2026, No. 2 behind Greystar). Growth is mostly acquired (FPI, about 165K units); organic growth was flat.
- Runs Yardi, RealPage and Entrata; markets itself as "PMS-agnostic". EliseAI is already deployed for leasing, maintenance intake, delinquency and renewals — do not rebuild resident chat.
- Site payroll is paid by owners; corporate functions (transitions, accounting, BD) are the manager's own cost.
- PMS write access is restricted. Prefer read-only or file-in/file-out designs.
- Stay out of rent pricing (algorithmic-pricing litigation).
- Chief Transformation Officer Cory Hitte owns technology and the AI roadmap; there is no CTO.
- Research facts carry their own confidence labels. Do not present inferred or vendor-claimed figures as verified.

## Working rules for the prototype

- Treat **Friday, October 16, 2026** as "today" (the data pack's as-of date, business day 6 of the September close). Owner reports are due business day 8, Tuesday, October 20.
- The data pack is synthetic and deliberately messy. Never fix data silently: show what was changed and what was held back for a human.
- Code computes every number; a model writes language only. Each figure must trace to a data-pack row.
- When the data cannot explain something, say so and state the question a human should ask.
- The tool advises and never acts. Drafted emails, holds and approval requests are proposals for human approval; nothing is sent, paid or posted.
- It only has to run locally for a screen-share demo. No Docker, databases or deployment work.
- Brand: the palette in `tailwind/tailwind.config.js` (cream `#f8f6f3`, deep blue `#154571`, navy `#0c1827`), read from assetliving.com. Plus Jakarta Sans stands in for the site's licensed fonts.
- Read the .xlsx with `openpyxl` in Python (installed in user site-packages; `pandas` is not installed).
